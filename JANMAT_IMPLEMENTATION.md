# JANMAT Implementation — Participatory Infrastructure Budgeting

## 1. Vision & Tagline

> **"Your neighbourhood. Your priorities. Your money."**

JANMAT bridges the gap between municipal data and resident priorities. Rather than allocating road repair funds in top-down opacity, JANMAT empowers residents to vote on evidence-backed infrastructure proposals and suggest unaddressed neighborhood needs.

---

## 2. Proposal Generation Methodology

Proposals are deterministically generated by scanning multi-modal civic datasets:
- **School Access Corridors**: Triggered when schools are within 250m and open pothole/paver complaints exist.
- **Hospital Emergency Routes**: Triggered on arterial/bus routes near healthcare facilities with historical accident records.
- **Stormwater & Flood Mitigation**: Triggered on roads intersecting flood hotspots or in wards with low-lying vulnerability $\ge 0.65$.
- **DLP Forensic Audit**: Triggered on roads with repeat repair failures within 30 days under active Defect Liability Period.
- **Road Rehabilitation**: High-traffic corridors with base course degradation.

---

## 3. The 8 Independent Prioritization Dimensions

No opaque black-box AI scores. Every proposal is evaluated along 8 transparent dimensions (0–100):

1. **Public Priority**: Resident upvotes and community complaint volume.
2. **Infrastructure Urgency**: Structural hazard level (cave-in: 100, open manhole: 90, pothole cluster: 65).
3. **Safety Impact**: Accident history and BEST bus route traffic exposure.
4. **Facility Accessibility**: Proximity to hospitals (+45 pts) and schools (+25 pts).
5. **Financial Feasibility**: Proportion of available ward budget consumed.
6. **Technical Feasibility**: Execution feasibility using rate-card supported techniques.
7. **Environmental & Flood Resilience**: Low-lying index and flood hotspot proximity.
8. **Evidence Confidence**: GPS accuracy and cross-referenced work-order history.

---

## 4. Lifecycle & Governance Model

```
Draft → Evidence Review → Technical Review → Open for Voting → Voting Closed 
      → Budget Review → Approved / Deferred / Rejected → Tender Preparation 
      → In Progress → Completed → Citizen Verification
```

Every transition is recorded with timestamp, actor name, role, and formal administrative rationale.
