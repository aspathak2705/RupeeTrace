# Data Limitations & Provenance Policy

## 1. Synthetic Nature of Data

All data used in RupeeTrace is synthetic and generated for the **Claude Impact Labs Hackathon**. It is designed to model the complexity, friction, and multi-layered relationships of Mumbai's civic infrastructure without compromising real municipal security or citizen privacy.

### Key Rules
- **Recorded Billed Cost $\neq$ Verified Payment**: The field `cost_inr` represents the contractor's billed amount recorded on a work order. It does **not** signify that the municipal treasury or bank has disbursed funds.
- **No Treasury Bank Records**: The dataset does not contain bank ledger scrolls, RBI payment slips, or electronic fund transfer logs.
- **No Raw Material Invoices**: There are no supplier delivery challans, bitumen grade test certificates, or asphalt batch plant batching logs.
- **No Physical Photographs**: While citizen complaints contain photographic reference tags, no actual JPEG/PNG image files are provided.
- **Historical Context Only**: Rainfall, high tide, and traffic congestion values represent historical baseline scenarios rather than live streaming telemetry.
- **Illustrative GeoJSON Boundaries**: Road and ward geographical polygons are simplified vector representations for analytical prototyping.

---

## 2. Evidence Grounding & Provenance Tracking

To prevent AI hallucination and ensure audit accountability, RupeeTrace enforces source attribution on every displayed record:

```json
{
  "provenance": {
    "source_file": "work_orders.csv",
    "source_record_id": "WO25-00600",
    "row_index": 599
  }
}
```

Every user-facing screen displays an `EvidenceBadge` linking directly to the source file and record ID.

---

## 3. Screening Thresholds vs Legal Conclusions

Findings generated by the **Repair Auditor** (such as rate-card deviations $>20\%$ or potential duplicate work orders) are **automated screening indicators**. They are intended to direct engineer attention to files requiring verification of physical Measurement Books (MB) and site inspection sheets. They do **not** constitute legal determinations of corruption or contractual fraud.
