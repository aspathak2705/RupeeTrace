# Civic Value Optimizer — The Unifying Intelligence

## 1. Overview

The Civic Value Optimizer generates an explainable, 13-point investment decision brief that unifies the entire RupeeTrace platform into one cohesive record.

---

## 2. The 13 Foundational Questions Answered

Every proposed capital investment generates a report answering:

1. **Why is this project needed?** (Problem statement and justification)
2. **What evidence supports it?** (Complaints, facilities, traffic index, flood hotspots)
3. **What do citizens think?** (JANMAT resident upvotes and community sentiment)
4. **How urgent is it?** (Infrastructure hazard score and vulnerability ranking)
5. **Is it technically feasible?** (Standard engineering methods vs specialized review)
6. **What is the indicative cost and budget fit?** (Itemized cost breakdown vs ward balance)
7. **What alternative engineering/material approaches exist?** (IRC/MoRTH alternatives and 5-yr lifecycle savings)
8. **Can existing DLP obligations cover any of the work?** (Contractor warranty enforcement check)
9. **Which contractors have relevant category experience?** (Empirical track record in this repair type)
10. **What procurement criteria should be considered?** (Pre-declared quality and technical standards)
11. **What are the major execution risks?** (Traffic disruption, monsoon rainfall, utility cables)
12. **How will citizens verify the work?** (7-stage verification timeline and QR-linked Road Passport)
13. **How will success be measured?** (Objective metrics: zero reopenings in 90 days, rapid flood drainage, roughness index)

This brief transforms civic infrastructure planning from arbitrary patronage into transparent, verifiable science.
